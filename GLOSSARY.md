# OpenPood

A Windows voice-dictation utility for turning speech into text at the active cursor.

## Language

**OpenPood**:
The current product and brand name.
_Avoid_: OpenPud, except when identifying the former product in upgrade documentation.

**Dashboard**:
The workspace for dictation shortcuts, usage, history, dictionary, and settings.

**Onboarding**:
The four-step welcome and setup flow for a new user.

**Recording widget**:
A temporary, non-interactive indicator of recording and processing status.

**Dictate**:
The mode that turns speech into text in the spoken language.

**Translate**:
The mode that turns speech into text in the user's selected target language.
